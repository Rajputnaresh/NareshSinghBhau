## Users

Two overlapping audiences evaluate this page, often on mobile, in seconds:

1. **Recruiters & hiring managers** screening for senior revenue leadership roles (Head of Sales, Regional Manager, GTM leadership, Key Account leadership). They need quick, credible proof of scope, scale, and promotion trajectory.
2. **Founders / CXOs** evaluating whether Naresh should run their revenue org or advise on B2B SaaS & revenue strategy. They need proof of P&L ownership, go-to-market mechanism, and operator depth.

Both audiences' job is the same: verify the track record fast and decide whether to reach out. Confirmed by owner.

## Product Principles

1. **Evidence over adjectives.** Every headline claim maps to a verifiable figure or achievement; nothing is asserted that the resume can't back.
2. **One spine, two audiences.** The same facts serve recruiters (scale, promotions, retention) and founders (P&L ownership, GTM, advisory) without branching into separate stories.
3. **Seconds, not minutes.** The evaluation is fast and often on mobile; legibility and structure rank above decoration.
4. **Accuracy is a hard constraint.** Site copy never drifts from the owner-confirmed figures or the PDF resume.
5. **Conversion is the point.** Download, contact, and connect must stay one tap away from any decision point.
